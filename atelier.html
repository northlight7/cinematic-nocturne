One scent,
made slowly.

Most houses launch a family of fragrances at once. This one launches a single composition and keeps working on it.

The formula is short by design: bergamot, rose, jasmine stem, cedar, amber and moss. Nothing is there to fill space.

01

One perfumer, one brief, and a long time spent on the transition from rose to cedar.

02

The concentrate rests for six weeks before it is diluted.

The bottle
is reusable.

The glass is thick enough to refill and the cap is metal, not plated plastic. A refill costs less than a new bottle, which is the only way a refill scheme works.

The label is paper because a paper label can be replaced, and a printed bottle cannot.
