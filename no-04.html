How it
opens.

Bergamot first, sharp and a little bitter. Then the rose arrives, and it is not a clean rose: there is stem and earth in it. Three hours later, cedar and a trace of something resinous.

The bloom on the opening page is the composition, not a decoration. Each note changes the light around the bottle.
